# areaMD
A tool for doctors to quickly gain a picture of how someone's address affects their health. Factors such as air pollution and water quality may go unnoticed while quietly causing someone's symptoms.
It aids faster diagnosis and streamlines appointments by providing extra context for doctors to gain a holistic understanding of patients.

## Description
When patients' symptoms can be caused by a diverse range of causes, it takes time for doctors to identify what may be causing them, and they make mistakes. 
Living factors can cause health problems that are not obvious on first glance. With this web app, doctors can add their patients, and save data about their home, 
work and previous addresses, to see what might be causing problems now, or with even more hidden damage, what previous exposure may be still causing harm. Even when
doctors ask patients about how their living area has affected their health, patients may not know themselves. Using governmental and other reliable sources of data, 
we analyse postcodes, considering factors such as air quality, water quality, and proximity to construction sites, as well as taking into account any news (such as recent chemical spillages or smog)
that might have had an effect on a patient. 

## Getting started
Access https://areamd.onrender.com/, where a doctor with suitable credentials can log in and view their patients.
